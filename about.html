Writing worlds at the age of thirteen

Ksitiz P. Subedi is a teenage author from CG Villas, Kathmandu, who accomplished something remarkable in 2025: the publication of his first three books at the age of thirteen — before most people his age had even thought about writing one.

Writing across three entirely different genres — memoir, fractured fairy tale, and high fantasy — he has already caught the attention of the international literary world. Two of his books carry blurbs from Joel Havenstone, acclaimed author of Choice of Rebels, a recognition that speaks to the quality and ambition of his debut work.

"A hilarious, offbeat twist on a classic children's story. There's still an emperor tricked into wearing nonexistent clothes in public; but Ksitiz P. Subedi also throws in time-travelling twins, a dance test for the crown, a cunningly disguised android, and dozens of other unexpected quirks. I laughed out loud, and came away impressed by a young author stretching his storytelling skills in new directions."

— Joel Havenstone, author of Choice of Rebels

Ksitiz is only just getting started — driven by a quiet but unwavering goal: to become both a celebrated author and a genuinely good person.

Author's Note

Outside of my writing, I spend my time exploring new ideas, reading widely, and developing the worlds that will shape my future stories. My work is driven by curiosity, imagination, and a desire to create something meaningful at a young age. My next projects continue to expand the universe of Elnius and beyond.
